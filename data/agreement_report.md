# Agreement Report

## Dataset
The gold dataset contains 160 labelled items, with a 70/30 split between development and test sets.

- Dev: 112 rows
- Test: 48 rows
- Labels collected by labeler_1 and labeler_2 for every item

## Results
The double-labelling analysis uses percentage agreement and Cohen's kappa.

- Percentage agreement: 86.25%
- Cohen's kappa: 0.72

## Interpretation
The agreement is moderately strong. Most disagreements were due to borderline cases rather than clear mismatches, and these were resolved by the definitions in the labelling guide.
